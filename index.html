FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few things you might want to know before getting started.

What do I need to provide before you start? +
I'll need the content and information relevant to your website, such as your text, images, branding, and any specific features or references you'd like included. I'll guide you through anything else needed before the build begins.
Can I request changes during the build? +
Yes. You'll receive a live staging link to review the website as it's being built. You can provide feedback and request changes within the agreed project scope before launch.
Do I own the website once it's finished? +
Yes. Once the project is complete, the agreed website code and assets are handed over to you.
Do you provide hosting? +
Hosting can be discussed as part of your project. If you already have a hosting provider, I can work with your existing setup where appropriate.
How does payment work? +
Packages are purchased through Whop. After payment, I’ll get in touch to confirm your project details, required content and next steps before the build begins.
What if I need something outside the package? +
No problem. If your project needs additional pages, features, integrations, or functionality outside the listed scope, we can discuss a custom quote.
When does the turnaround time start? +
The typical turnaround begins once payment is confirmed and I’ve received all the required content, assets and project information needed to start the build. Delays in providing these materials or feedback may affect the timeline.
